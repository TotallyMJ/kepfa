Kepfa

Your data

Kepfa keeps the sky you notice. Without signing in, everything stays on this device — no account, no server. If you sign in to sync across devices, here is what we keep and the choices you have.

What we keep

Only when you sign in: the skies you keep — their photos, Live clips, notes, place and dates — the stamps you earn, and the email from your Google sign-in, so the jar is known to be yours.

Where it lives

On Supabase servers, sent over HTTPS. Each person can reach only their own skies. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone.

Location

If you allow location, a rounded position (about 10 km) is used to guess your place and the weather behind today’s jar. Your exact position never leaves this device, and shared images are redrawn with no GPS in them.

Your choices

“Save a copy” downloads all your skies as one file, anytime. “Delete my cloud data” removes your synced skies, photos and stamps from the server. Not signing in keeps everything on this device only.

Keeping it

Synced data stays until you delete it. Questions about your data: kepfa@vibethebloom.com.
